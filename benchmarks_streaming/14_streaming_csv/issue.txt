Repair read_rows(chunks), decode_chunks(chunks), and parse_records(parts). Input chunks are arbitrary bytes including empty chunks; decode UTF-8 incrementally, strip only an initial BOM, and raise UnicodeDecodeError for invalid or incomplete UTF-8. decode_chunks yields text fragments; parse_records accepts arbitrary text fragments and yields lists of fields. CSV uses comma delimiters, double-quoted fields, doubled quotes as escapes, and CR/LF/CRLF record separators. Preserve CR/LF literally inside quoted fields. Unquoted empty fields are None; quoted empty fields are empty strings. Preserve whitespace. Quotes may start only at field start; after a closing quote only delimiter, record separator or EOF is valid. Malformed quotes raise ValueError. Skip blank physical records outside quotes, preserve trailing empty fields, and accept a final record without a terminator. Chunk boundaries must not affect results. All helpers remain lazy: yield each complete row before requesting the next input chunk or parsing later text, including when later text in the same chunk has malformed CSV syntax. Do not collect the entire stream. The three helper contracts are public; preserve them.
